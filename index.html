How it works

  1. You tell me what you need and who will read it.
  2. I read the public material and send you my questions.
  3. You receive the finished piece within the agreed time.
  4. One round of revisions is included.

Technology analysis based on public information. It is not investment advice. Confidential material is handled only under an agreement.
